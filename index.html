Wi-Fi 射频八项测试指南Wi-Fi RF Eight-Test Guide

八项核心收发测试的详细步骤、判定标准与常见坑（中英双语）Detailed steps, pass criteria and pitfalls for the 8 core TX/RX tests (bilingual)

01 · TX

Transmit power 发射功率Transmit power

测量发射信号强度（dBm）：校线损、逐信道/速率扫描、判定与补偿。Measure TX level (dBm): cable-loss calibration, per-channel/rate sweep, pass criteria.

 02 · TX

Spectrum mask 发射频谱模板Transmit spectrum mask

验证带内外能量分布符合标准模板，防止带外泄漏干扰邻道。Verify out-of-channel energy fits the standard mask; prevent adjacent-channel interference.

 03 · TX

Modulation accuracy 调制精度 (EVM)Modulation accuracy (EVM)

解调信号算星座点误差，决定最高可用速率，最能暴露射频问题。Demodulate and compute constellation error; sets the top usable MCS; most revealing test.

 04 · TX

Frequency error 发射频率误差Transmit frequency error

测载波频偏（ppm），反映晶振质量与 AFC 校准。Carrier offset in ppm; reflects crystal quality and AFC calibration.

 05 · TX

Center frequency leakage 中心频率泄漏Center frequency leakage

测 OFDM 直流子载波上的本振泄漏，反映 IQ 调制器与 DC offset 校准。LO leakage on the OFDM DC subcarrier; reflects IQ modulator and DC-offset calibration.

 06 · TX

Spectral flatness 频谱平坦度Transmit spectral flatness

测带内子载波功率起伏，发现滤波器滚降、频偏、信道估计问题。In-band subcarrier ripple; reveals filter roll-off, frequency offset, channel-estimation issues.

 07 · RX

RX min sensitivity 最小输入灵敏度RX minimum input sensitivity

反向灌包找 PER=10% 的最弱电平，衡量接收机"耳朵最灵"。Sweep downlink power to find the weakest level at PER=10%.

 08 · RX

RX max input level 最大输入电平RX maximum input level

反向灌强信号验证接收机不饱和，保证近距离仍能通信。Flood the receiver with a strong signal to verify it does not saturate.

通用前提Common prerequisites

  • 线损校准：所有传导测试前，用网分/功率计测出各频点线缆损耗并输入测试软件补偿，否则全部数据失真。
  • Cable-loss calibration: before any conducted test, measure cable loss per frequency (VNA/power meter) and compensate in the test software; otherwise all numbers are wrong.
  • 测试模式：用芯片厂工具（wlu / art2 / MP tool 等）使 DUT 脱离协议栈，进入连续发射/接收模式。
  • Test mode: use the chip vendor tool (wlu / art2 / MP tool…) to take the DUT out of the protocol stack into continuous TX/RX modes.
  • 屏蔽箱：隔绝环境 Wi-Fi 干扰，对灵敏度测试尤其关键。
  • Shield box: isolate ambient Wi-Fi; critical for sensitivity tests.
  • 仪器：Wi-Fi 综合测试仪（IQxel、CMW270 等）或频谱仪 + 信号源组合。
  • Instruments: a Wi-Fi combo tester (IQxel, CMW270…) or a spectrum analyzer plus vector signal generator.
  • 限值以 IEEE 802.11 标准与典型产品规格为例，实际以对应标准版本和客户规格书为准。
  • Limits shown follow IEEE 802.11 and typical product specs; defer to the exact standard revision and your customer spec.
